1412.6087 Stringy effects in scrambling

by Stephen H. Shenker, Douglas Stanford

Prerequisites

Shockwave geometries and the butterfly effect, Kabat-Ortiz eikonal quantum gravity.

Summary

They evaluate the 4-point function

$$D(\{t_i,x_i\}) = \big\langle V_{x_1}(t_1)\, W_{x_2}(t_2)\, V_{x_3}(t_3)\, W_{x_4}(t_4) \big\rangle \tag{7}$$

where all four operators act on the right boundary, there is no implicit time ordering, and $V, W$ are approximately local single-trace operators that raise the energy of the thermal state by $\sim \beta^{-1}$. One then imposes OTOC kinematics, i.e. the $V$ insertions at

$$(x_1,t_1) \approx (x_3,t_3)$$

and the $W$ insertions at

$$(x_2,t_2) \approx (x_4,t_4)$$

with $V$ and $W$ separated by a time scale much larger than the thermal time, i.e.

$$t_1 - t_2 \gg \beta$$
