2505.08116 de Sitter JT gravity from double-scaled SYK

by Kazumi Okuyama

Prerequisites

dS basics, JT gravity-matrix model basics, DSSYK basics.

Summary

Okuyama hints at yet another way of doing dS$_2$ holography out of the Double-Scaled-Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (DSSYK) model. Recall in DSSYK the energy spectrum was

$$E(\theta) = -E_0\cos\theta$$

where $E_0 = 2\mathcal{J}/\sqrt{1-q}$, with $\mathcal{J}$ the variance of the SYK couplings and $q = e^{-\lambda}$.

The lower edge of the spectrum $\theta \to 0$ is a stable saddle which gives AdS$_2$-JT gravity in the triple scaling limit $\lambda \to 0$, $\theta \to 0$ with $k = \theta/\lambda$ fixed (3.17). Okuyama essentially shows the unstable saddle $\theta = \pi$ gives dS$_2$-JT in the triple scaling

$$\lambda \to 0, \quad \theta \to \pi \quad \text{with} \quad k = \frac{\pi - \theta}{\lambda} : \text{fixed} \tag{4.1}$$

The proof methodology is simple:

1. Get the DSSYK disk partition function from the literature:

$$\mathcal{Z}(\beta) = \int_0^\pi \frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\, \mu(\theta)\, e^{-\beta E(\theta)} \tag{2.4}$$

with $\mu(\theta) = (q, e^{\pm 2i\theta}; q)_\infty$.

2. Express it as an ETH matrix integral ensemble average in the $L \to \infty$ limit:

$$\mathcal{Z}_{0,1}(\beta) = \lim_{L \to \infty} \frac{1}{L} \big\langle \operatorname{Tr} e^{-\beta H} \big\rangle, \qquad \big\langle f(H) \big\rangle = \frac{1}{\mathcal{Z}} \int dH\, e^{-L \operatorname{Tr} V(H)} f(H) \tag{2.14, 2.15}$$

where $L = 2^{N/2}$, the normalization is $\mathcal{Z} = \int dH\, e^{-L \operatorname{Tr} V(H)}$, and the potential $V(H)$ is fixed by matching the genus-zero eigenvalue density of the matrix model to $\mu(\theta)$.

3. Take the triple scaling.

4. Realize you obtained the JT gravity partition function. Around $\theta = \pi$ the Gaussian comes out with the wrong sign

$$\tilde{Z}_{0,1}(\tilde{\beta}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi(-\tilde{\beta}/\gamma)^3}}\, e^{\frac{2\pi^2\gamma}{\tilde{\beta}}}, \qquad (\tilde{\beta} = -\beta < 0) \tag{4.6}$$

with $\gamma = 1/(E_0\lambda^2)$ — exactly the disk amplitude of de Sitter JT gravity.

The above eqns are for the disc but has been done for arbitrary genus in the main paper.

He also constructs an explicit solution in sine dilaton gravity whose ADM Mass matches the DSSYK spectrum and shows that the metric becomes negative AdS$_2$ i.e. equivalent to dS$_2$ in the triple scaling limit.
