2505.08116 de Sitter JT gravity from double-scaled SYK

by Kazumi Okuyama

Prerequisites

dS basics, JT gravity-matrix model basics, DSSYK basics.

Summary

Okuyama hints at yet another way of doing dS$_2$ holography out of the Double-Scaled-Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (DSSYK) model. Recall in DSSYK the energy spectrum was

$$E(\theta) = -E_0\cos\theta$$

where $E_0 = 2\mathcal{J}/\sqrt{1-q}$, with $\mathcal{J}$ setting the variance of the random SYK couplings, $\langle J_{i_1 \cdots i_p}^2 \rangle = \mathcal{J}^2 \binom{N}{p}^{-1}$, and $q = e^{-\lambda}$ where $\lambda = 2p^2/N$ is held fixed in the double-scaling limit.

The lower edge of the spectrum $\theta \to 0$ is a stable saddle which gives AdS$_2$-JT gravity in the triple scaling limit $\lambda \to 0$, $\theta \to 0$ with $k = \theta/\lambda$ fixed (3.17). Okuyama essentially shows the unstable saddle $\theta = \pi$ gives dS$_2$-JT in the triple scaling

$$\lambda \to 0, \quad \theta \to \pi \quad \text{with} \quad k = \frac{\pi - \theta}{\lambda} : \text{fixed} \tag{4.1}$$

The proof methodology is simple:

1. Get the DSSYK disk partition function from the literature:

$$\mathcal{Z}(\beta) = \int_0^\pi \frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\, \mu(\theta)\, e^{-\beta E(\theta)} \tag{2.4}$$

where $\mu(\theta) = (q, e^{\pm 2i\theta}; q)_\infty$ is the DSSYK density of states.

2. Take the triple scaling.

3. Realize you obtained the dS JT gravity disk partition function. Around $\theta = \pi$ the Gaussian comes out with the wrong sign

$$\tilde{Z}_{0,1}(\tilde{\beta}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi(-\tilde{\beta}/\gamma)^3}}\, e^{\frac{2\pi^2\gamma}{\tilde{\beta}}}, \qquad (\tilde{\beta} = -\beta < 0) \tag{4.6}$$

with $\gamma = 1/(E_0\lambda^2)$.

The above eqns are for the disc but has been done for arbitrary genus in the main paper by realizing DSSYK can be expressed as an ETH matrix integral ensemble average in the $L \to \infty$ limit:

$$\mathcal{Z}_{0,1}(\beta) = \lim_{L \to \infty} \frac{1}{L} \big\langle \operatorname{Tr} e^{-\beta H} \big\rangle \tag{2.14}$$ $$\big\langle f(H) \big\rangle = \frac{\int dH\, e^{-L \operatorname{Tr} V(H)}\, f(H)}{\int dH\, e^{-L \operatorname{Tr} V(H)}} \tag{2.15}$$

where $L = 2^{N/2}$ is the dimension of the Hilbert space of the $N$ Majoranas. Matching the disk partition functions of DSSYK and ETH matrix model or equivalently a spectral matching $\rho_0(E(\theta))\,dE(\theta) = \mu(\theta)\,d\theta/2\pi$ fixes the form of the potential

$$V(H) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n}\, q^{\frac{n^2}{2}} \big(q^{\frac{n}{2}} + q^{-\frac{n}{2}}\big)\, T_{2n}(H/E_0) \tag{2.13}$$

with $T_n$ the Chebyshev polynomial of the first kind, and the higher genus partition functions follow via topological recursion just like in Saad-Shenker-Stanford.

He also constructs an explicit solution in sine dilaton gravity whose ADM Mass matches the DSSYK spectrum and shows that the metric becomes negative AdS$_2$ i.e. equivalent to dS$_2$ in the triple scaling limit.
