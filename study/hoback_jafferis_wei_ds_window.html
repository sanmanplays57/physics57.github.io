2606.31705 Monitoring a de Sitter universe through an anti-de Sitter window

by Sarah Hoback, Daniel L. Jafferis, Zixia Wei

Prerequisites

AdS/CFT basics, BCFT basics.

Summary

Within BTZ spacetime

$$ds^2 = -\frac{4}{(1+uv)^2}\,du\,dv + r_h^2\,\frac{(1-uv)^2}{(1+uv)^2}\,d\phi^2 \tag{1}$$

lies a locally dS$_2$ hypersurface

$$u(v) = -\frac{1+\alpha v}{\alpha + v}\,, \qquad u < v \tag{2}$$

whose holography is studied in this paper. If the theory is pure AdS$_3$ gravity with the above hypersurface acting as an EOW brane $Q$ with $R_Q \sim T^2 - 1$ so that $T > 1$ is dS$_2$, then there exist no real Euclidean saddles with the EOW brane reaching a non-trivial asymptotic boundary, but there are complex saddles.

Pure Euclidean AdS$_3$ i.e. brane tension $T = 0$ is conformal to a half ball $\rho \in (-\infty, 0]$: adding the brane adds a cylinder of length $\rho_*$ to the end of the half ball with $T = \tanh\rho_* <1 $. Complexifying $\rho$ gives access to $T > 1$ and then using GKP-W the gravitational path integral is performed first with disk and then with cylinder as the asymptotic boundary.

There are two solutions associated with two branches of the analytic continuation of $\rho$

$$\rho = \operatorname{arccoth} T \pm i\frac{\pi}{2} \equiv \rho_*^{\pm} \tag{12}$$

which leads to two boundary conditions (boundary states $|B\rangle$ and $|\bar{B} \rangle$) on the two boundaries of the cylinder. Due to time reflection symmetry, only the case with two different boundary states contribute, and calculating the holographic energy-momentum tensor reveals that the scaling dimension is negative:

$$h^{B\bar{B}}_{\rm bcc} = -\frac{c}{8}$$

So it is a unitary CFT with a non-unitary boundary condition (in the sense that the expansion of the boundary state in terms of Ishibashi states has complex coefficients) or in other words: in the closed string channel it is unitary but not in the open one.
